Our Philosophy

Cooking that follows
the season, not the trend.

Ambre began as a single table in a converted wine cellar, built on one idea: let the ingredient lead. Every dish on our menu changes with what the morning markets bring in — nothing frozen, nothing rushed.

Our kitchen is small on purpose. It lets us taste every plate before it leaves the pass, and hold a standard we'd be comfortable serving in our own homes.

Julien Moreau Executive Chef & Founder
